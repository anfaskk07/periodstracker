💻 How Step 1 Works (Beginner Code Breakdown)

Ee app-inte 3 main parts enthokkeyaanu cheyyunnathu ennu nokkaam:

1. HTML (The Skeleton / ഘടന)

Creates the input boxes (`<input>`) for Age, Weight, Height, and Last Period Date, plus the empty card containers where the daily Kerala meals and water glasses appear.

2. Tailwind CSS (The Design / ഭംഗി)

Styles the cards with soft rose and green colors, rounded corners (`rounded-2xl`), and responsive grids (`grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3`) so it works smoothly on mobile and laptop screens.

3. JavaScript (The Brain / ബുദ്ധി)

Calculates BMI, daily water need (`weight × 35 ml`), next period date, and multiplies base Kerala food & fruit quantities (grams/cups) to match your exact body profile.

4. Step 2: Calendar & Remedies

Uses `new Date(year, month, 1)` to build a 7-column calendar grid, colors each date by cycle phase, and maps logged symptoms to Kerala kitchen remedies (`KERALA_SYMPTOM_REMEDIES`).

5. Step 3: Grocery & History

Aggregates weekly grain, green, fruit, and seed grams scaled by body weight, and stores multi-cycle records in `localStorage` with print/copy support.
